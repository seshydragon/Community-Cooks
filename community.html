Cook beyond your kitchen

Food is
community.

Find ways to turn cooking into something that helps people around you.

01

Food drives

Organize a shelf-stable food collection with your school, neighborhood, club, or community group. Use Community Cooks to plan recipes around what you already have.

02

Volunteer

Look for local food pantries, meal-packing projects, and food rescue groups that accept teen volunteers. Always check the organization's age requirements before signing up.

03

Community challenges

Run a group cooking challenge: choose one ingredient, cook a meal, and share what you learned. The goal is participation, not ratings.

04

Bring people in

Invite friends to the project. A future version can connect accounts, shared challenges, and a live community leaderboard without filling the site with fake activity.

Built to grow

Start local. Build outward.

Community Cooks currently keeps progress on the device. A future backend can safely connect real community members and verified events.
